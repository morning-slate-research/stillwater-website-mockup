The first credible paid product

A property water diligence brief - not a county “security” grade.

Broad county scores make tidy marketing but weak decision support. A specific property brief can identify the relevant public-record trail, surface unresolved questions, and tell a buyer when they need title, legal, engineering, or hydrogeology help.

01

Frame the property and decision

Location, intended use, local authority, and what is actually being purchased.

02

Link the public record

Relevant well, permit, district, drought, and water-rights materials - with sources, dates, and limits.

03

Separate facts from the next question

No legal or investment conclusion. Clear red flags and the right expert follow-up.
