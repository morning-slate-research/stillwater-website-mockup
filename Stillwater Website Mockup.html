Field note

A water story is rarely a single map layer.

Aquifers, permits, wells, districts, rights, and land tenure speak different languages. Stillwater’s job is to put the record in order before a buyer, agent, lawyer, or specialist takes the next step.

Ownership record

Ownership is the story that a risk map cannot tell.

Public land and water data describes conditions. Stillwater’s editorial layer asks a second question: who is acquiring the ground, rights, or operating position connected to those conditions?

Water conditions need rights, use, and local-authority context.

Aquifers, permits, districts, wells, and land tenure do not all mean the same thing. The useful output is an attributable starting point, not a false universal score.
